Map notes

Pan and zoom to browse. Labeled points are verified landmarks with brief, well-documented facts. Your own exact spot doesn't come with historical notes — the label just marks where you are.

Nearby help

Real, community-mapped places near you — no paid map key involved. Coverage depends on how well your area is mapped on OpenStreetMap.

Elevation is estimated from your coordinates using a public terrain-elevation dataset (Open-Meteo), typically accurate to within a few meters on open ground. Reference elevations are commonly cited figures for each summit or landmark and can vary slightly by source. Air pressure and boiling point are computed from a standard-atmosphere formula for your elevation only — actual weather conditions shift both. Map tiles and place search are from OpenStreetMap/CARTO and Open-Meteo geocoding. Anything you add or select resets when you reload the page.
